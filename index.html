How we teach

The Spark Method

Three principles that turn capable speakers into confident academic readers and writers.

01

Structured Literacy & Phonics

Students learn to decode words systematically — mastering the 44 English sounds instead of guessing word shapes from memory.

02

High-Feedback Small Classes

Small groups of just 4–6 students let teachers give frequent, specific feedback — 20–25 correction moments per child in a single lesson.

03

Low-Anxiety Boutique Environment

A warm, close-knit villa setting helps children participate, make mistakes and improve with confidence — never lost in a crowd.
