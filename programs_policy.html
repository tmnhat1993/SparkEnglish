Good to Know

Enrollment Terms & Policies

Clear answers on payment, attendance, make-up sessions and classroom policies — so there are no surprises once your child is enrolled.

  1. Home
  2. /Programs
  3. /Enrollment Policies

Thank you for considering Spark English Center for your child's education. This page summarizes our policies on payment, attendance, classroom rules and communication for both in-person and online classes.

Quarterly Billing

Invoices settle within 7 days. 10–15% discounts for 6- and 12-month advance payment.

2 Make-Up Classes/Month

Completed within 30 days, with 8 hours' advance notice.

A "No Sugar" School

No sugary snacks or caffeine in the 90 minutes before class.

Max 6 Per Class

Private lessons available by teacher evaluation.

Payment

  • Payment for classes is required quarterly and must be settled within 7 days of receiving the invoice.
  • Attendance may be restricted if payment is not received by the 10th day.
  • 6-month advance payments receive a 10% discount. 12-month advance payments receive a 15% discount.
  • No refunds are issued for missed classes or withdrawals.

Class Attendance & Make-Up Sessions

  • Students are allowed a maximum of two make-up classes per month, completed within 30 days of the missed class.
  • At least 8 hours' advance notice is required during business hours (8am–6pm, Monday–Saturday) to qualify for a make-up session.
  • For online classes, students arriving more than 10 minutes late without advance notice may not be admitted; teachers wait up to 15 minutes before the session becomes ineligible for a make-up.
  • A minimum commitment of 3 hours per week (in person or online) is required.

School Policies

  • Spark is a "no sugar" school — sugary snacks are not permitted in the classroom, and we recommend avoiding sugar and caffeine for 90 minutes before class, as both measurably affect focus and performance.
  • Snacks brought in against this policy will be returned to students after class.
  • Group classes do not exceed 6 students.
  • Private lessons are offered based on teacher evaluation and availability; private students may be merged into a group class at the teacher's discretion.

Communications

Parent responsibilities: ensure your child can access the ClassDojo portal for assigned homework. Coordinate scheduling, payment and administrative matters through Sabrina Do or Kieu Nguyen (sparkalcvn@gmail.com · 0398 143 487).

Contact points: teachers communicate with parents directly through ClassDojo. Spark's administrative team reaches parents via Viber, WhatsApp or Zalo.

Discounts

Referrals: a one-time 10% discount on the first month's fee for any new student who enrolls in a quarterly program through your referral — with no cap on the number of referrals.

Additional fees: materials fees are billed separately at cost. Siblings receive a 5% discount on class fees.

Agreement

By enrolling your child at Spark English Center, you agree to these terms and conditions. If you have any questions, please contact us — we're glad to help.

Common Questions

FAQs About Enrollment

How often is payment due?

Payment is required quarterly and must be settled within 7 days of receiving the invoice. Attendance may be restricted if payment isn't received by the 10th day.

Are advance payment discounts available?

Yes — 6-month advance payments receive a 10% discount, and 12-month advance payments receive a 15% discount.

How do make-up classes work?

Students are allowed up to two make-up classes per month, completed within 30 days of the missed class, with at least 8 hours' advance notice during business hours (8am–6pm, Monday–Saturday).

Why is Spark a "no sugar" school?

Research shows sugar and caffeine measurably affect focus and performance in lessons. We ask families to avoid sugary snacks and caffeine for 90 minutes before class.

How will I communicate with my child's teacher?

Teachers communicate directly with parents through ClassDojo. Spark's administrative team is reachable by Viber, WhatsApp or Zalo for scheduling and payments.
