In short

What this program covers

  • Who it's for: students developing English communication skills, from complete beginner to advanced.
  • The problem it solves: gaps in everyday communication skills that hold back confidence and classroom participation.
  • The approach: 14 structured levels, moving through all four language skills together.
  • The outcome: confident, practical communicators ready for IB English or IELTS preparation next.

Where Students Get Stuck

Some students haven't yet built a broad enough English foundation to keep up with everyday classroom instructions, casual conversation, or simple reading and writing tasks. Without a structured plan, progress happens unevenly — strong in speaking, weak in writing, or the reverse — and gaps compound over time.

The Spark Approach

Fourteen Levels, One Consistent Method

Students progress through 14 structured levels that build speaking, listening, vocabulary, grammar, reading and writing together — not in isolation. Lessons focus on meaningful communication: real conversation, practical vocabulary and grammar used in context, not memorized in a vacuum.

Classes of 4–6 mean every student speaks, is heard, and gets corrected in every lesson — the same small-class model behind every Spark program.

What Students Learn

Speaking & Listening

Speaking confidence, pronunciation, listening comprehension and natural conversational flow.

Vocabulary & Grammar

Practical vocabulary and grammar taught in context — used in real communication, not drilled in isolation.

Reading & Writing

Foundational reading and writing skills that grow alongside speaking and listening, not behind them.

Common Questions

FAQs

How does Spark know which of the 14 levels my child should start at?

A free Spark Readiness Assessment places your child at the right level using speaking, listening, reading and writing tasks — not just age or grade.

How long does it take to move up a level?

It varies by student, but most students move through a level in one to two terms of consistent lessons, faster with regular practice at home.

Is this program only for beginners?

No. The 14 levels span complete beginner through advanced, so students already speaking English confidently can still join at a level that stretches their grammar, vocabulary and writing.

What happens after level 14?

Students who complete the ESL pathway typically move into Spark's IB English or IELTS Success Program, depending on their age and goals.
