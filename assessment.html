Before You Book

FAQs

We understand parents may have questions before booking an assessment. Here are answers to the most common ones about the process, and what to expect.

How long does the assessment take?

Most assessments take around 30–45 minutes, depending on the student's age and English level, guided through speaking, reading, phonics and listening activities in a calm environment.

What skills are included in the assessment?

Speaking, pronunciation, reading, phonics, listening, grammar and comprehension — adjusted based on the student's age and English level.

Does my child need to prepare?

No preparation is needed. We want to see your child's natural, current ability so we can recommend the right starting point.

How will I know which class is right for my child?

After the assessment, we'll give you clear, written feedback on strengths, areas for improvement, and a specific recommended class or program.

Will parents receive feedback?

Yes. Every assessment ends with clear feedback for parents — not just a placement, but an explanation of what we found and why.

Can shy or nervous students still complete the assessment?

Absolutely. Our goal is not to "test" children — it's to help them feel comfortable and supported. Teachers adjust their approach for quieter students.

Is the assessment online or in person?

Both options are available. In-person assessments take place at our Thao Dien center; online assessments are conducted over video call.

What ages do you assess?

We assess students from age 3 through secondary school and university-bound learners preparing for IELTS.
