Before You Reach Out

FAQs

How quickly will Spark respond?

We reply to messages and enquiry forms within one business day. For an urgent question, calling 0398 143 487 is fastest.

Can I visit Spark without an appointment?

We recommend booking a free assessment or calling ahead so a teacher can give you their full attention and show you around properly.

I want to enroll — is this the right form?

For enrollment, a free assessment is the best first step — it tells us where to place your child. Use the assessment form instead, or select "Enrollment Questions" above and we'll guide you.
