In short

About the community

  • Thao Dien has more international and bilingual schools per square kilometer than anywhere else in Vietnam.
  • Spark is a specialist English center, not a general tutoring franchise — built for exactly this neighborhood's needs.
  • We support students from BIS, ISHCMC, EIS, AIS, Aurora, EtonHouse and schools across the wider District 2 area.

Thao Dien is not just a neighborhood for international school families — it is the center of gravity. Within a radius of roughly two kilometers of Spark, there are more international and bilingual schools per square kilometer than anywhere else in Vietnam. Spark was built here because this is where the need is most concentrated, and most specific: a specialist English center that fills the gap standard curriculum-aligned, academically rigorous English support delivered within walking distance of the schools these children attend.

The Thao Dien School Cluster

British International School HCMC (BIS)

22B Nguyen Van Huong, Thao Dien · British curriculum (IGCSE, A-Levels), part of Nord Anglia Education · ~1,400 students from 50+ nationalities

English Demands

Structured assessment through IGCSE and A-Levels; academic English requirements rise sharply from Year 3, with an emphasis on reading comprehension and analytical writing.

Where Spark Helps

Students in EAL who need faster progress. Students preparing for IGCSE English. Students whose writing has plateaued at B1 needing IELTS for university applications.

International School of Ho Chi Minh City (ISHCMC)

28 Vo Truong Toan, Thao Dien · IB curriculum (PYP, MYP, DP), one of the largest established IB World Schools in Vietnam

English Demands

The IB's inquiry-based approach places heavy demands on academic English — students must read critically, write analytically and express complex ideas from early in the MYP.

Where Spark Helps

Students transitioning from PYP to MYP who need stronger academic writing. Students preparing for IB English and Literature at DP level.

European International School HCMC (EIS)

730 Nguyen Van Huong, Thao Dien · IB curriculum (PYP, MYP, DP); a smaller, village-style campus

English Demands

Similar profile to ISHCMC families. The smaller school environment means less outside resource internally.

Where Spark Helps

Families who value outside specialist support alongside a close-knit school community.

Australian International School Saigon (AIS)

36 Thao Dien Road (Thao Dien campus), plus Tan Phong and Xi campuses · Australian curriculum with IGCSE and IB Diploma options

English Demands

Students navigating the IGCSE/IB pathway choose which route best fits their English strength.

Where Spark Helps

Students at the Thao Dien campus who need after-school support within the neighborhood.

Aurora International School

11-13 Tran Ngoc Dien, Thao Dien · Reggio Emilia-inspired early years, New Zealand curriculum for primary and middle school

English Demands

Children transitioning from Reggio-inspired early years into structured primary need systematic phonics development.

Where Spark Helps

Children preparing for a transition into BIS, ISHCMC or another school at Year 1.

EtonHouse International Pre-School Thao Dien

42 Street 10, Thao Dien · Reggio Emilia-inspired, IB PYP candidate

English Demands

Phonics and early literacy foundation before and alongside the transition to primary school.

Where Spark Helps

Children who need structured reading and writing foundations early.

Other schools in the wider District 2 area

Beyond the Thao Dien core, we also serve families from The American School (TAS), BVIS, Anne Hill, Saint Angela, La Petite Ecole, Saigon Star, Renaissance, ABC International School, and the network of bilingual schools across District 2 including Vinschool and Asian School campuses.

Why a Single Specialist Center Beats a Chain With Branches

There is a reason Spark has one center in Thao Dien and not branches across HCMC. The reason is quality control.

When an English center opens multiple branches, it needs to staff them. That means hiring more teachers, often at speed, often accepting a lower qualification threshold. The curriculum gets diluted as new staff interpret it differently. Quality varies between branches, and parents have no way to know which branch is strong and which is stretched.

Spark operates differently. We have one location, one teaching team, one consistent standard. Every student is taught by the same small group of specialist teachers. Every parent can speak directly to the director. Every class runs to the same standard because there is no second location where standards might slip.

Our center at 204B7/12 Nguyen Van Huong is within walking distance or a short drive of every school listed above. For most Thao Dien families, the commute is less than ten minutes.

What Thao Dien Families Ask Us

FAQs

My child goes to BIS/ISHCMC/AIS. Do they really need outside English support?

Many students at these schools speak English confidently but still need dedicated support with the academic reading, writing and vocabulary their IB or British-curriculum classes require. A free assessment tells you clearly, either way.

We just moved to Thao Dien. My child starts next term. What should we do?

Book a free assessment before term starts if possible. It gives us a clear picture of your child's level so we can recommend the right pathway from day one.

My child is at a bilingual school. Is Spark for international-school families only?

No. Spark supports both international and bilingual school families across Thao Dien and the wider District 2 area.

We live in District 7. Is it worth the commute to Thao Dien?

Many Spark families travel from beyond Thao Dien specifically because we are one specialist center, not a chain. We're happy to discuss scheduling that works for your family.

Where exactly is Spark located?

204B7/12 Nguyen Van Huong, Thao Dien, Ho Chi Minh City — within walking distance or a short drive of most schools in the cluster above.

Do you coordinate with my child's school?

We don't have formal partnerships, but we shape each learning plan around your child's specific school curriculum and are glad to review school feedback with you.

How do I get started?

Book a free Spark Readiness Assessment — a calm, one-to-one 30–45 minute session that tells us exactly where your child stands.
