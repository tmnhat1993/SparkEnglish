Our Story

Built to Close One Gap

Spark exists for a single reason: children who sound fluent in English but struggle the moment school asks them to read, write and think academically.

  1. Home
  2. /About
  3. /Our Story
In short

Our story, in four facts

  • Founded to close one specific gap: fluent speaking that doesn't transfer into academic reading and writing.
  • Built literacy-first. Reading and writing are treated as the foundation, not an afterthought to conversation.
  • One center, not a chain. A single Thao Dien location with one consistent teaching team.
  • Small by design. Classes of 4–6 students so every child is actually seen and corrected.

Why We Started

Most parents who first come to Spark describe the same puzzle: their child chats easily with friends, teachers and even strangers in English — yet comes home with a reading assessment below grade level, or an essay covered in corrections. It looks like a contradiction. It isn't.

Speaking and academic English are different skills built on different foundations. A child can master everyday conversation years before they master phonics, sentence structure, academic vocabulary or the kind of critical reading international and bilingual schools expect from Year 3 onward. Spark was founded to close exactly that distance — what we call the immersion trap — for the families it shows up for most: children in Thao Dien's international and bilingual schools.

A Literacy-First Belief

Many English programs are built around conversation, because conversation is the visible, confidence-building part of learning a language. Spark was built the opposite way. We start from the belief that reading and writing are the foundation academic success is actually built on — and that speaking confidence follows naturally once a child can decode, comprehend and construct language on the page.

That belief shapes everything downstream: our Structured Literacy and phonics foundation, our small class sizes, and the Phonics → IB English → ESL → IELTS pathway that carries a student from their first decoded sound to university-ready academic English.

One Center, By Design

When an English center opens multiple branches, it has to staff them — often quickly, often lowering the qualification bar, and inevitably diluting how consistently its method is taught from one location to the next. We made a deliberate choice not to do that. Spark operates from one villa in Thao Dien, taught by one small, consistent team, so every family gets the same standard — not a version of it.

What Hasn't Changed

The things we've never compromised on

  • Class size: 4–6 students, never a large group masquerading as "small."
  • The boutique setting: a home-like villa built for focus, not a commercial center built for volume.
  • The Thao Dien address: one location, walking distance from the schools we serve.
  • The literacy-first method: phonics and Structured Literacy before anything else.

Common Questions

FAQs About Spark

Is Spark part of a franchise or chain?

No. Spark is a single, independent specialist center in Thao Dien. We chose to stay one location with one consistent teaching team rather than open branches, so quality never varies by location.

Do you only work with international school students?

Most Spark families come from international and bilingual schools, because that is where the gap between conversational and academic English shows up most clearly. We also welcome any family who wants stronger literacy and academic English foundations.

Why is Spark based in Thao Dien specifically?

Thao Dien has one of the highest concentrations of international and bilingual schools in Ho Chi Minh City. Spark was built here so families can reach specialist English support within a short walk or drive of school.
