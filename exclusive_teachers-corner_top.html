Teacher's Corner

Insights From Spark Educators

  1. Home
  2. /Exclusive
  3. /Teacher's Corner

Every week, Spark teachers work with students developing reading, writing, vocabulary and academic English skills. Along the way, we see common challenges, recurring questions and important learning breakthroughs that many families experience.

Teacher's Corner is a collection of articles, practical advice and educational insights written by Spark educators to help parents better understand how children learn — whether your child is learning phonics, developing reading fluency, improving writing skills, or preparing for IELTS.

What You'll Find in Teacher's Corner

Reading Development

Why children guess words, how phonics creates fluent readers, reading age, helping reluctant readers, and choosing the right book.

Writing Development

Why students struggle with writing, building better sentences, paragraph writing, and supporting writing at home.

Vocabulary & Academic English

Why vocabulary predicts academic success, academic vs. conversational English, and how students learn new words.

International School Success

Supporting IB and British-curriculum students, the IB Learner Profile, and managing reading demands at school.

IELTS & Future Pathways

When to begin IELTS preparation, common writing mistakes, and building reading skills for exam success.

New Every Month

Teacher's Corner is updated regularly with new articles, practical guides and classroom insights — written by educators who teach every day.
